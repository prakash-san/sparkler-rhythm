फुलझड़ी · Phuljhari

Sparkler Rhythm

Fireworks burst to the beat and drop Diwali gifts. Slide your basket under each gift to catch it. Red crackers drop too: let them pass, because catching one costs you two drops. Twelve diyas along the bottom light as the song plays.

Drag left and right to move the basket, or use the arrow keys. You can drop ten gifts. The tenth drop ends the game, and the diya up top counts how many you have left. Letting a cracker pass earns a small bonus.
